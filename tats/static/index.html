FOCI refresh-token flows

FOCI (Family of Client IDs) is signalled by a foci field in the Microsoft token-endpoint response. A refresh token from Family "1" can be redeemed at the token endpoint by any other client in that family — not just the app it was issued to.

BroCI / Nested App Authentication exchanges

BroCI is detected by brk_client_id / brk_redirect_uri form parameters or a redirect URI shaped like brk-<guid>://…. The broker's id never appears as a JWT claim — it only shows up on the wire.
